What is Jorvik Wishlist? A free fan-made helper for Star Stable Online (SSO) players. Browse every Star Stable horse breed and coat with its Star Coin price and where to buy it, plan which horses to save for, see what's coming to the next Traveling Bazaar and past Bazaars, check which horses come with events like Halloween, Winter Village and Summer Daze, keep a list of the horses and pets you own, and track how many Star Coins you need.

Unofficial fan-made site. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Star Stable Entertainment AB. Star Stable, its horses and artwork belong to Star Stable Entertainment AB. This site is free and has no ads.

Your wishlist, stable and Star Coins are saved only in this browser. Nothing is sent to a server (except suggestions you choose to send), and there are no accounts. Use 💾 Backup to move them to another device.

Data from official Star Stable news, the Pixel-Ponies Bazaar tracker, the Star Stable wiki and the Starstable Database.
