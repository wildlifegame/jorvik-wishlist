# Jorvik Wishlist

A free, unofficial fan-made site for Star Stable Online players: make a wishlist of horses and coats, see prices and where to buy them, track the Bazaar, keep a list of the horses you own, and share your wishlist with a link.

No accounts and no ads. Everything you add is saved only in your own browser.

## Files
- `index.html` – the whole site
- `data/catalog.json` – horses: price, level, shop, coat names
- `data/bazaars.json` – past and upcoming Bazaars (add new ones here)
- `img/coats/` – coat pictures (one strip per breed)
